# Home Office dimension and silhouette audit

Audit date: 2026-09-05. All dimensions below are **width × depth × height**, in millimetres, rounded to the nearest whole millimetre. Manufacturer tables often use W × H × D or L × W × H; those axes have been converted. Height never becomes top-view depth.

A generic category has no single correct physical size. The measured presets below use a specific reference product's outer dimensions while retaining simple generic blueprint shapes. They are not exact CAD silhouettes of every product of that size. Tables, mats, the ergonomic chair and articulated lamp remain explicit generic planning envelopes. The two speculative 34″ and 40″ regular-monitor options were removed; those diagonal sizes remain in the ultrawide family.

## Monitors

Width is the actual chassis chord width, not a width extrapolated linearly from diagonal. Overall depth includes the supplied stand. The screen curve, thin panel edge, rear electronics housing and stand are separate geometry. Curvature uses the reference radius; panel edge is a schematic 18 mm (not claimed as measured thickness). Without-stand depth includes curved sweep and housing, not just the panel. Base dimensions are schematic except the Dell bases whose published widths are available. The silhouette is centred in its outer footprint.

## Rendering and saved plans

- All 13 families use width/depth axes; PCs and speakers show their top casing, not front panels. No front-facing speaker cones, screen contents or open-laptop keyboard.
- The Apple mark uses Apple's navigation SVG, centred and uniformly scaled on both laptop lids and the Mac mini. It cannot stretch with just one object axis.
- Profiles survive save/load, duplication, resizing and variant changes. Older objects can recover a profile from their original preset name without changing saved dimensions. Renamed older objects without profiles use the generic fallback.
- Existing room positions, user sizes and saved defaults are not silently rewritten. Re-select a size in the object menu to apply its corrected dimensions and reset defaults.
- Collision/selection uses the enclosing footprint, not the empty spaces between chair legs or monitor feet. Cable plugs, operating clearances and chair recline are not included.
- Tests cover every preset at its native size and extreme narrow/wide resizes, profile serialization, equal thin-panel edges at 34″ and 57″, and logo aspect preservation.
