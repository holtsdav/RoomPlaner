# Instruments and framed pictures

These unbranded presets use representative outer dimensions in millimetres,
not specifications for a particular product. Check measurements of the actual
instrument or frame when planning a tight fit.

| Preset                                  | Width | Floor depth | Height |
| --------------------------------------- | ----: | ----------: | -----: |
| Full-size concert grand piano (88 keys) |  1600 |        2750 |   1000 |
| Electric piano (88 keys, with stand)    |  1400 |         400 |    850 |
| Full-size acoustic five-piece drum set  |  2000 |        1500 |   1400 |
| Acoustic guitar on floor stand          |   500 |         450 |   1150 |
| Acoustic guitar on wall mount           |   412 |         200 |   1038 |

The drum footprint includes cymbals and throne in a compact arrangement. Pedals are omitted from the schematic drawing. Piano envelopes exclude
bench and player clearance. Guitar envelopes include the stand or wall offset.
Drawings are schematic top views; upright guitars are viewed from above, not
as front elevations. The wall guitar starts 900 mm above the floor.

Framed pictures offer outer width × height sizes of 10 × 15, 13 × 18,
21 × 29.7, 30 × 40, 40 × 50, 50 × 70, 60 × 90 and 70 × 100 cm.
Frame depth is a representative 30 mm. Sizes describe the outer frame rather
than an opening or mat. Every size is initially centered at 1500 mm above the
floor. The picture's height is metadata, not floor-plan depth; existing wall
attachment and size controls apply. Elevations do not simulate vertical collisions.

Both guitars share a 412 mm wide, 118 mm deep body based on the
[Yamaha FG-series specifications](https://usa.yamaha.com/products/musical_instruments/guitars_basses/ac_guitars/fg_series/fg_800/specs.html)
(1038 mm total instrument length). The stand envelope and mounting clearance
remain generic estimates; they no longer stretch the instrument body. The
foreshortened neck and strings are schematic. The drum throne is moved 120 mm
forward within the existing footprint.
