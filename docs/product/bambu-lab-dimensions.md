# Bambu Lab printer dimensions

Added to Home Office as the **Bambu Lab 3D Printer** family. Dimensions are
manufacturer physical machine envelopes in millimetres, ordered width × depth ×
height. They are not build volumes or package sizes.

| Model     | W × D × H (mm)  | Manufacturer source                                                                                     |
| --------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A1 mini   | 347 × 315 × 365 | [A1 mini](https://ca.store.bambulab.com/products/a1-mini)                                               |
| A1        | 465 × 410 × 430 | [A1 technical specifications](https://bambulab.com/pl/a1/tech-specs)                                    |
| P1P       | 386 × 389 × 458 | [P1P](https://eu.store.bambulab.com/en-se/products/p1p)                                                 |
| P1S       | 389 × 389 × 458 | [P1S](https://us.store.bambulab.com/collections/3d-printer/products/p1s)                                |
| P2S       | 392 × 406 × 478 | [P2S manufacturer specification sheet](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/cb24b13a195541199f3f820270a7df3b.pdf) |
| X1 Carbon | 389 × 389 × 457 | [X1 Carbon](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/x1-carbon)                                           |
| H2S       | 492 × 514 × 626 | [Bambu Lab buying guide](https://bambulab.com/it/support/buying-guide)                                  |
| H2D       | 492 × 514 × 626 | [H2D](https://eu.store.bambulab.com/products/h2d)                                                       |

These presets represent standalone printers. Allow additional space for AMS / AMS
lite, external spools, cables, waste collection, ventilation, open doors and lids,
and moving beds (especially A1 and A1 mini). No operating-clearance envelope is
included. Top-view internal details are schematic, with separate cantilever,
bed-slinger, open CoreXY and enclosed forms; they are not manufacturer CAD drawings.
