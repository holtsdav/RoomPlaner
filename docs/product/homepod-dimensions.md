# Apple HomePod dimensions

Verified against Apple on 5 September 2026. Both objects live in Home Cinema, alongside the other speakers, as separate library entries.

| Object                                                                          | Apple width × depth × height | Stored footprint × height |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| [HomePod (2nd generation)](https://www.apple.com/homepod-2nd-generation/specs/) | 142 × 142 × 168 mm           | 142 × 142 × 168 mm        |
| [HomePod mini](https://www.apple.com/homepod-mini/specs/)                       | 97.9 × 97.9 × 84.3 mm        | 98 × 98 × 84 mm           |

The document stores whole millimetres. Mini rounds by +0.1 mm in width/depth and −0.3 mm in height. The circular top-down silhouette fills the stored physical envelope in previews, canvas and exports, including after resizing. Touch surface and volume markings are schematic interior details. No cable or operating clearance is added to the physical size.
